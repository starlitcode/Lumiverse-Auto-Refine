# Contributing

Thank you for wanting to help with Auto Refine.

## Bug reports, not pull requests

Please send a bug report or an idea instead of a pull request. I do not accept pull requests. Every change to the code goes through me.

A clear bug report helps me more than a pull request. Questions and ideas are welcome too.

## Where to send it

- Post in my [Auto Refine post](https://discord.com/channels/1484149939167821915/1545884657743171594) on the Lumiverse Discord server, or
- [open an issue](https://github.com/starlitcode/Lumiverse-Auto-Refine/issues).

For anything sensitive, such as a security problem, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What to put in a bug report

I fix a bug from what the report says. If a report is short or vague, I cannot tell what went wrong or how to fix it. Please include:

1. What you did, step by step.
2. What you expected to happen.
3. What happened instead. Copy any message word for word.
4. Whether you are on a phone or a computer, and which browser.
5. A screenshot or a screen recording, if it shows the problem.

## The debug report

Auto Refine can build a report for you to paste in. You do not have to use it. It does help me see exactly what your problem is, because it shows your version, your settings and what the extension did.

1. Open the settings panel and go to the **Log** tab.
2. Find the **Reporting a problem** card.
3. Under **What it carries**, choose what to include.
4. Press **Read and edit it first**, and take out anything you would rather keep private. Or press **Copy it** to copy it as it is.
5. Paste it into your report.

What your prompt blocks say is never included. Nothing leaves your device until you paste it. [The Log tab](docs/settings.md#log) explains each part.

## The two branches

- `testing` gets every change first, including new and experimental features.
- `stable` is what most people install. It only moves when a version is released, so it can be behind `testing`.

If you installed from `testing`, say so in your report.
